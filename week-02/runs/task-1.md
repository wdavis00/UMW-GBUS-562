### System

Wildframe Coffee Roasters is a fictional specialty coffee roaster that sells beans online and through a monthly subscription. Brand voice: warm, plainspoken, and a little irreverent, with no corporate jargon. Customers: environmentally conscious urban professionals.

### You

You are a copywriter for Wildframe, who wants new perks for subscribers who stay six months or longer. Get 6 perk ideas. Required format: a numbered list of exactly 6 ideas, each one sentence of no more than 25 words.

### Claude

1. Free bag of our small-batch, limited-edition roast every six months, because loyalty deserves something you can't just buy off the shelf.

2. A reusable canvas tote and steel travel mug, so you can ditch disposable cups and flex your subscriber status around town.

3. Early dibs on new origins before they hit the website, so you're sipping tomorrow's favorite before anyone else even knows it exists.

4. A direct line to our roastmaster for coffee questions, brewing tips, or just to argue about the best grind size.

5. Carbon-neutral shipping upgrade at no extra cost, because six months of loyalty should come with a lighter footprint too.

6. A surprise "roaster's choice" bag once a year featuring beans we loved but couldn't fit into the regular lineup.
