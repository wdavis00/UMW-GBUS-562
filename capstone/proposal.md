Problem: When people begin projects, improvement efforts, or AI interactions without adequately framing the underlying problem, they lose time, create rework, preserve unsupported assumptions, and may pursue solutions that do not address what they actually need.

Who uses it: Any individual working through a business or personal-work concern, opportunity, idea, or proposed solution, typically at project initiation and again whenever new information changes their understanding.

Techniques: Prompt engineering and Team. Prompt engineering guides solution-neutral elicitation, reflection, adaptive follow-up, and synthesis. A Team separates conversational problem framing from independent review of the draft problem statement so framing weaknesses can be surfaced before the user confirms it.

Human oversight: Draft-only, because the tool helps the user develop and articulate a problem frame, but the user remains the authority who reviews, corrects, and confirms that the result accurately represents their current understanding.
