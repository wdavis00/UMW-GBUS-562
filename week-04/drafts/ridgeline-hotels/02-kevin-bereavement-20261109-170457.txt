Hi Kevin,

I'm sorry to hear about the loss of your grandmother.

Under our Bereavement and Family Leave policy, the death of a grandparent qualifies for up to 3 paid days off. These days don't need to be taken consecutively, but they must be used within 60 days of the event. This leave is paid and does not reduce your PTO or sick balances (source: bereavement-and-family-leave.html).

To use this leave, just let your supervisor, Scott Bailey, know as soon as you can - no documentation is required.

If you need any additional time beyond the 3 days, please check with People and Culture, as the bereavement policy itself only covers up to 3 paid days for a grandparent.

Wishing you and your family comfort during this time.
