I'm very sorry about the loss of your grandmother.

Here's what our bereavement leave policy provides:

- For the death of a grandparent, you're entitled to up to 3 paid days off. These days don't need to be taken consecutively, but must be used within 60 days (source: bereavement-and-family-leave.html).
- No documentation is required — just let your supervisor (Scott Bailey) know as soon as you can.
- This bereavement leave does not reduce your PTO or sick balance, so your current PTO (10 days) and sick days (6 days) remain untouched.

If you need additional time beyond the 3 paid days — for example, for travel to Albuquerque — you're welcome to use PTO for the extra day(s).

Please let Scott know your plans, and reach out to People and Culture if you have any other questions.
